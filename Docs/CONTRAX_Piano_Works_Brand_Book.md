# About This Document

This Brand Book defines the identity of CONTRAX Piano Works and serves as the foundation for every project contained in this repository.

Rather than creating unrelated analytics projects, this portfolio follows one fictional company through every stage of its supply chain—from sourcing raw materials and manufacturing pianos to inventory management, production planning, logistics, and executive reporting.

All information in this document is fictional and created for educational and portfolio purposes.

---

# Company Story

CONTRAX Piano Works is a fictional American manufacturer of premium acoustic and digital pianos.

The company was created to combine two of my interests: piano and supply chain management.

As someone who enjoys playing piano and is pursuing a degree in Supply Chain & Logistics Technology, I wanted to build a portfolio around a business that I genuinely find interesting while demonstrating real-world manufacturing, operations, procurement, inventory, and analytics concepts.

Throughout this portfolio, CONTRAX Piano Works serves as the fictional company behind every dataset, dashboard, report, and analysis.
