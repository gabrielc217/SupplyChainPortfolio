## About

CONTRAX Piano Works is a fictional American manufacturer of premium acoustic and digital pianos created as the foundation of my supply chain analytics portfolio.

I built this company to combine a personal interest in piano with my academic and professional interests in manufacturing, operations, and supply chain management.

Every dataset, dashboard, SQL query, and Python project in this repository supports one connected business rather than a collection of unrelated projects.

## Product Naming Philosophy

Every piano is named after a musical term or composition form.

Examples include:

- Nocturne
- Ballade
- Prelude
- Étude
- Sonata
- Rhapsody
- Reverie
- Arabesque

Limited edition instruments may honor influential composers whose work helped shape piano performance.

## Manufacturing Philosophy

CONTRAX Piano Works combines traditional craftsmanship with modern manufacturing and data-driven decision making.

Every project in this portfolio reflects one or more core supply chain functions, including procurement, inventory management, production planning, logistics, and business analytics.
